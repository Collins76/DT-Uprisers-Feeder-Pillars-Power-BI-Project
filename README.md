
# DT-Uprisers-Feeder-Pillars-Power-BI-Project

## A brief Introduction and Overview:

The Power BI dashboard(https://app.powerbi.com/links/_RYdXGSsJP?ctid=581816b2-c0be-4eaa-80e3-29e5f2486494&pbi_source=linkShare) I have created for Ikeja Electric Plc provides a comprehensive monitoring system for electricity distribution infrastructure, with a particular focus on upriser and feeder pillar inspections across various business units in the Lagos area. Overall, the dashboard successfully combines operational metrics, geographical data, and performance indicators to support infrastructure management and maintenance decisions. The multi-page approach allows for different analytical perspectives while maintaining a cohesive theme.

### Dashboard Design and Layout:

![Image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3ece493-e24f-40c3-a8e8-cf9a62224cf4)

The dashboard employs a well-organized grid layout that effectively compartmentalizes different data categories. The consistent color scheme—primarily using green for positive indicators and red for negative ones—creates visual harmony while ensuring intuitive interpretation. The segmentation between key metrics, field officer performance, and business unit analysis provides a logical flow of information.

The use of cards for headline metrics (7,647 good uprisers, 1,101 bad uprisers, etc.) effectively highlights the most important figures. However, some areas appear slightly crowded, particularly in the field officers and public DTs sections, which could benefit from additional white space or hierarchical organization.

### Data Visualization Analysis:

#### The dashboard employs a diverse and appropriate mix of visualization types:

The line graph tracking good versus bad uprisers over time effectively shows trends and patterns
The pie charts for upriser condition (87.44% good, 12.59% bad) and feeder pillar condition (64.6% good, 24.0% excellent, 7.0% poor, 2.2% critical, 2.2% no assessment) provide immediate proportion insights
The map visualization adds valuable geographic context, showing the distribution of inspections across Lagos with color-coding by business unit
The horizontal bar charts comparing business unit performance allow for quick ranking and comparison
The "Good Upriser Percentage Over Time" graph effectively combines trend lines for average performance with actual daily data points, though the y-axis scaling could be improved for better readability. The color consistency between charts helps maintain visual cohesion throughout the dashboard.

### Key Metrics and Insights:

#### The dashboard presents several critical operational metrics:

Upriser Performance: 7,647 good uprisers (87.44%) versus 1,101 bad uprisers (12.59%) out of 8,513 total inspections
Record Validation: 4,663 valid records versus 171 invalid records
Distribution Transformer Tracking: 4,834 total captured DTs
Field Officer Performance: Notable variations in productivity and quality, with officers like Mrs. Omoloyosi Esther Oluwole achieving 58.01% good upriser rates across 65 inspections, while others like Mrs. Uchechukwu Agatha Nneke completed 121 inspections
Business Unit Analysis: Akowonjo BU has the highest number of inspections (1,843) while Oshodi BU shows 2,014 with a high proportion of good uprisers
Feeder Pillar Conditions: Predominantly good (64.6%) or excellent (24.0%), with smaller percentages rated poor (7.0%) or critical (2.2%)
The "Upriser Condition by Business Unit" visualization effectively highlights performance disparities between business units, with the green/red bars showing good/bad upriser proportions. This provides clear targets for improvement efforts.

### Data Quality and Completeness:

#### Several data quality issues are evident that should be addressed:

Multiple "NaN" values appear in the field officer performance table, particularly in the "Avg. Good Upriser %" column
Some business units show disproportionately high numbers of inspections, which may indicate data collection inconsistencies
The validation metrics (4,663 valid vs. 171 invalid records) suggest data quality processes are in place, but the 3.5% invalid rate indicates room for improvement
Implementing data validation rules and addressing missing values would enhance the dashboard's reliability for decision-making.

### Interactive Features:

#### The dashboard includes several interactive elements that enhance its utility:

Date range filters (visible in the bottom section) allow temporal analysis
Business unit selectors enable focused analysis on specific regions
The map provides interactive geographical filtering
The tabs at the bottom (Upriser_FeederPillar_Dashboard, Upriser_FeederPillar_Dashboard-2, Undertaking Performances) enable different analytical views
Adding tooltips with additional context information and implementing cross-filtering between visualizations would further enhance the interactive experience.

### Business Implications and Actionable Insights:

#### The dashboard reveals several business-critical insights:

The 12.59% bad upriser rate represents a significant maintenance opportunity, with potential implications for system reliability and customer service
Performance variations between field officers (from 0.75% to 58.01% good upriser rates) suggest opportunities for training, process standardization, or best practice sharing
Business unit performance disparities highlight potential regional maintenance challenges or resource allocation issues
The 9.2% of feeder pillars in poor or critical condition should be prioritized for maintenance to prevent potential failures
Undertaking performance varies significantly, with Dopemu UT (515), Orile Agege UT (469), and Iju UT (235) showing the highest DT counts

These insights can drive targeted maintenance programs, resource reallocation, and performance improvement initiatives.

### Conclusion:

Your Power BI dashboard for Ikeja Electric Plc provides a robust foundation for monitoring and managing electricity distribution infrastructure across Lagos mainland areas. Its strengths lie in comprehensive data coverage, effective visualization variety, and clear presentation of key operational metrics.

The dashboard successfully balances operational detail with strategic overview, enabling both day-to-day management and longer-term planning. It has the potential to drive significant improvements in infrastructure maintenance, field officer performance, and ultimately, electricity distribution reliability for Ikeja Electric's customers.
